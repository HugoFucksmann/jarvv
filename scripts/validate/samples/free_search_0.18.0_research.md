**France**,<sup>[\[j\]](https://en.wikipedia.org#cite_note-20)</sup> officially the **French Republic**,<sup>[\[k\]](https://en.wikipedia.org#cite_note-21)</sup> is a country primarily located in [Western Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Europe).[\[l\]](https://en.wikipedia.org#cite_note-22)[Its overseas regions and territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_France) include [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana) in [South America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_America), [Saint Pierre and Miquelon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Pierre_and_Miquelon) in the [North Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Atlantic), the [French West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_West_Indies), and [many islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_islands_of_France) in [Oceania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania) and the [Indian Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean). [Metropolitan France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France) shares borders with [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) and [Luxembourg]
